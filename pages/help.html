📋 Frequently Asked Questions

1. Apna product/service kaise list karun?
▼
Login karne ke baad "Add Service" button par click karein. Apna product name, category, price, description aur image upload karein. Submit karne par aapki listing live ho jaayegi.
2. Payment kaise karta hai platform?
▼
Hum Cash on Delivery (COD), Online Card Payment, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), aur Wallet payments support karte hain. Sare online payments encrypted aur secure hain.
3. Order cancel ya refund karna hai
▼
Order place karne ke 24 ghante ke andar "My Purchases" page se cancel request bhej sakte hain. Online payments ke liye refund 3-5 business days mein process hoga. COD ke liye koi charge nahi.
4. Password bhool gaya — kya karun?
▼
Abhi ke liye, support form bharo aur "Account" topic select karo. Humari team aapki registered email pe reset link bhejegi. Password reset feature jaldi launch hoga.
5. Machine ki delivery kaise hogi?
▼
Order confirm hone ke baad service provider directly aapse contact karega. Zyaatar cases mein machine aapke address par deliver ki jaati hai. Location aur distance ke hisaab se timing vary kar sakti hai.
6. Kya seller ko rate kar sakte hain?
▼
Haan! Service milne ke baad "My Purchases" se order pe click karein aur rating de sakte hain. Aapki rating platform ki quality maintain karne mein help karti hai.

Aur questions ke liye right side ka support form use karein — hum 24-48 ghante mein reply karte hain.
